# JAL TELECOM — design direction

## Connecting the future

A confident engineering identity built around the connection between people, physical infrastructure and possibility. The visual language combines cinematic telecom imagery, fine circuit lines, clear editorial typography and authentic field photography.

### Foundations

- Brand navy: `#0B3D91`
- Brand cyan: `#00AEEF`
- Brand orange: `#FF7A00`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Supporting midnight: `#05172F`; text: `#12233C`; mist: `#F3F7FC`
- Typeface: Poppins, with a system sans-serif fallback
- Large, tightly spaced headlines; readable body text; small uppercase section labels
- Spacious desktop composition, responsive single-column mobile layouts
- Orange for primary actions, cyan for connections, navy for technical authority

### Homepage composition

1. Compact white navigation with the supplied JAL mark, language indicator and project enquiry action.
2. Cinematic full-width navy hero: “Engineering a more connected Angola.” Images dissolve into one another; they never travel sideways. Provide manual scene controls and pause. Stable headline keeps the main message readable.
3. A compact strip describing deployment, integration and ongoing support without fabricated performance metrics.
4. White editorial introduction paired with JAL's own crew photograph.
5. An asymmetric services composition: radio/site infrastructure as the lead service, supporting services in a clean grid.
6. An interactive technical connection graphic explaining the relationship between sites, transmission and operational support.
7. Selected field photography, accurately described as supplied field imagery, without invented customers, dates, outcomes or project contracts.
8. People and working approach, grounded in the supplied JAL crew images.
9. A direct project enquiry section and complete Luanda contact information.

### MVP information architecture

Home · About · Services · Individual service details · Field work · Contact · Privacy

All content begins in English. Portuguese is a later content milestone; no working language switch is implied before that version exists.

### Interaction and accessibility

Keyboard-accessible navigation, native dialog lightbox and service information, focus states, reduced-motion support, hero pause, no horizontal overflow, descriptive image text and honest email/WhatsApp enquiry actions. No simulated form delivery.

### Content boundaries

The branding sheet lists site installation, fibre optics, radio and transmission, maintenance and support, enterprise solutions, energy and infrastructure, and projects/consulting. These inform draft service copy, subject to business review. No unverified client logos, certifications, statistics, founding date, testimonials, uptime guarantees or coverage promises are included. Photos showing prominent competitor branding are excluded from the initial public-facing selection. AI-created visuals are conceptual illustrations, not photographs of JAL projects.

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Prompt: original cinematic telecom mast with microwave dishes above an abstract coastal African city at blue hour, cyan fibre light trails, navy/cyan/orange palette, subject on the right and dark negative space on the left, no text, logos or watermark.

Asset: `dist/assets/hero-connectivity.webp`. The image is illustrative and does not establish a JAL project location.

### Field photography

Fill gallery frames edge to edge on desktop and mobile. Choose each crop around the people and equipment that tell the story, keeping group portraits head to toe and retaining antennas, cables, and crew at height. Extend the surrounding scene when cropping would remove important details. Keep asset edit notes in the delivery report.
